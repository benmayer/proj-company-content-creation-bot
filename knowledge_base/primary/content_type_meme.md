# Meme content-type profile

Team editorial direction for meme drafts. Channel-specific rules live in each `channel_*.md` file under `## Meme`. It is guidance on how to write, not a company fact.

## Voice rules

- Cold, dry and knowingly cheeky rather than warm or apologetic.
- For delays, make light of the situation and the shared inconvenience instead of apologising for it.
- No emoji.

## Format rules

Two lines. Line 1 is the on-image text. Line 2 describes the picture in square brackets, for example `[image: a toddler staring over the seat back]`. Never joke about protected or vulnerable people.
