# Route and promotion patterns

## Common announcement structure

Official Ryanair announcements commonly combine:

1. a strong headline announcing a new route, aircraft, base, seasonal schedule, or airport milestone;
2. a concrete place and travel period;
3. operational proof such as routes, frequencies, aircraft, seats, investment, passengers, or jobs;
4. a customer benefit such as more low-fare choice, city breaks, winter sun, or connectivity;
5. a time-limited booking call to action through the Ryanair website or app when a sale is actually available;
6. a quote from Ryanair or a local airport representative in longer corporate releases.

## Source-backed examples

- The [Newcastle Summer 2026 announcement](https://corporate.ryanair.com/news/ryanair-adds-3rd-aircraft-at-newcastle-for-summer-2026/) combines an additional based aircraft, four new routes, increased frequencies, local investment/jobs, and a limited-time sale with a stated fare and booking channel.
- The [Budapest Summer 2025 announcement](https://corporate.ryanair.com/news/ryanair-launches-4-new-routes-and-record-budapest-summer-schedule-in-response-to-governments-scrapping-of-aviation-tax/) connects a policy development to a schedule, new destinations, investment, jobs, and a stated seat sale.
- The [Winter 2026 schedule announcement](https://corporate.ryanair.com/news/ryanair-launches-record-winter-2026-schedule-with-80m-seats-across-europe/) frames seasonal travel through winter sun, ski trips, Christmas markets, and city escapes, then gives route and seat details.
- The [Tirana Summer 2026 announcement](https://corporate.ryanair.com/news/ryanair-adds-4th-based-aircraft-in-tirana-for-summer-2026/) uses a local-market headline, concrete route and aircraft numbers, local jobs, and a short booking sale.

## Generation rules

- Never carry a fare, route, date, aircraft count, seat count, job number, or “new” claim into a new draft without the corresponding source and location.
- Use a promotion call to action only when the input includes a verified booking URL or source-backed offer window.
- Keep the customer benefit concrete: destination, timing, choice, connectivity, or a clearly sourced fare.
- If the request is about a current event or policy, separate the event from Ryanair’s response and label uncertainty rather than implying causation.
