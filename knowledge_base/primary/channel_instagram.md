# Instagram channel profile

Team editorial direction for Instagram drafts. It applies the platform cues in [brand_voice.md](brand_voice.md) and the examples in [past_content/examples_instagram.md](past_content/examples_instagram.md). It is guidance on how to write, not a company fact.

## Tone

Playful, visual-first and self-aware. Relatable travel moments told in the first or second person ("me", "you"), with a punchline rather than an explanation.

## Format

A short caption built around one recognisable travel or flight moment. Never a dense corporate explanation or a joke that needs a paragraph of context.

## Post

- A caption of one or two short lines, lowercase is fine.
- Describe the picture or moment the caption goes with in one line at the end, in square brackets: `[image: …]`.
- No hashtags. No emoji.

## Reply

- A reply to a comment: one lowercase line, normally 3–12 words.
- Answer the specific comment with a playful comeback; a little warmer than on X, still never an apology or a customer-service line.
- No emoji, no hashtag. Tease the situation, never the person.

## Meme

- The on-image text is one lowercase line in a "me when…", "POV:…", "when you…" or "that one…" format, 15 words at most.
- Pick a moment every flyer recognises (boarding, landing, security, seatmates, holiday plans) and exaggerate it.
