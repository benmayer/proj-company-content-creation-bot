# Company profile

## Identity

- The company is Ryanair, an Irish airline group headquartered in Dublin.
- Ryanair's public LinkedIn company profile lists the company as founded in 1985 and describes Ryanair Holdings plc as the parent of Ryanair DAC, Lauda, Buzz, and Ryanair UK.
- The public company profile describes the group as an airline and aviation business focused on low-cost travel, holidays, car rental, technology, and related travel services.

## Business model and positioning

- Ryanair's sustainability report describes the group as a low-fares, low-cost, short-haul airline group that expands its model across new and existing routes.
- The same report identifies customer service performance, punctuality, customer satisfaction, and operational performance as material customer-facing themes.
- Safe content framing: describe the value proposition as low-fare choice and connectivity only when the specific route, fare, date, or offer is supplied by an approved source. Do not invent fares, schedules, savings, or availability.

## Approved content facts

- Use “Ryanair” for the company unless a source specifically requires a group or subsidiary name.
- Keep facts tied to a dated source. Route counts, aircraft, passenger numbers, fares, jobs, schedules, and financial figures are time- and location-sensitive.
- Treat “Europe’s No.1 airline” and similar superlatives as source language, not as an invitation to create new rankings or unsupported comparisons.
