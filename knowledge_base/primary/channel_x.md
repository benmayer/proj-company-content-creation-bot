# X channel profile

Team editorial direction for X drafts. It applies the platform cues in [brand_voice.md](brand_voice.md) and the examples in [past_content/examples_x.md](past_content/examples_x.md). It is guidance on how to write, not a company fact.

## Tone

Fast, deadpan and topical. It sounds like one sharp person running the account, not a press office. Brutally honest and witty about the low-cost reality.

## Format

Lead with the point and keep the wording compact. The link to flying or Ryanair must be obvious without explanation.

## Post

- One or two short lines, 25 words at most. Lowercase is fine.
- One observation about a recognisable flying moment, or a topical hook with a clear travel angle.
- No hashtags, no emoji, no "book now" call to action unless the request supplies a fare or link.

## Reply

- One lowercase sentence, normally 3–12 words.
- Answer the actual post: pick the one concrete detail in it (the seat, the bag, the delay, the fee) and turn it into a deadpan comeback.
- Loose grammar and abbreviations like "tbf" are fine if they make it feel native.
- Never apologise, never sound like customer service, no emoji, no hashtag.
- Tease the situation or the poster's own choice, never their body, looks, family or anything personal.

## Meme

- The on-image text is one lowercase line in a "me when…", "POV:…" or "when you…" format, 15 words at most.
- It names a moment every flyer recognises; the picture does the rest.
