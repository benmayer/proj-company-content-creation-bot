# Ryanair on Instagram: verbatim examples

Source: E. Bakomitrou, *When Laughter Takes Flight: Analyzing Ryanair's Humorous Strategy on Instagram* (Erasmus University Rotterdam, 2024), [thesis.eur.nl/pub/75053](https://thesis.eur.nl/pub/75053/). The thesis analyses 100 Ryanair Instagram posts from 14 October 2023 to 10 April 2024 and quotes the text inside each meme verbatim; it excludes the separate post captions. The picture descriptions below are the thesis's, shortened.

No verified examples of Instagram post captions or comment replies have been collected yet, so only the Meme section exists. Add sourced examples under `## Post` or `## Reply` when the team collects them.
