# Ryanair on X: verbatim examples

Collected on 23 October 2026 from press coverage that quotes or embeds the original posts. The wording is Ryanair's. Any number or claim inside an example was true only on its date and is not a current company fact. Use these for tone, length and rhythm only.

## Reply

Each example shows the post Ryanair replied to, then the reply.

- Post: a groom's photo from his honeymoon flight, "I'm hitting home runs with flights for my wedding … Off on honeymoon now about to fly into #ibiza for the first time", sitting in a row with no window.
  Reply: "She's regretting marrying someone who can't read the fine print" (3 May 2023) — [Eye of the Flyer](https://eyeoftheflyer.com/2023/05/04/ryanair-trolls-honeymooning-newlyweds-twitter/)
- Post: "LOOOOOOOOL Rynair admin your days are numbered." (a commenter under the same thread)
  Reply: "like this marriage" (May 2023) — [Eye of the Flyer](https://eyeoftheflyer.com/2023/05/04/ryanair-trolls-honeymooning-newlyweds-twitter/)
- Post: a passenger complaining about the legroom.
  Reply: "come with your own plane next time ?" (8 May 2024) — [Hespress](https://en.hespress.com/84840-ryanair-tells-complaining-customer-get-your-own-plane.html)
- Post: someone claiming Ryanair planes rarely take off.
  Reply: "we only had 2,148 flights take off yesterday tbf, Jason" (15 Feb 2022) — [One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/insights/ryanair-twitter/)
- Post: a broadcaster telling Ryanair to "stick to poor quality budget travel, leave the politics to others."
  Reply: "Stick to poor quality broadcasting, leave the politics to others." (15 Feb 2022) — [One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/insights/ryanair-twitter/)
- Post: someone asking whether Ryanair planes have USB outlets.
  Reply: "yeah, they're just under the seat massage function." — [One Mile at a Time](https://onemileatatime.com/insights/ryanair-twitter/)

Too far, do not imitate: a passenger posted "Where's my WINDOW SEAT?" and Ryanair replied "Pay for the baggage you've stored under your jumper & we can talk" (11 May 2023). It was criticised as fat-shaming the passenger — [LBC](https://www.lbc.co.uk/article/ryanair-hits-back-after-being-accused-of-fat-shaming-passenger-DWzXyW_2/). Joke about the seat, the fee or the choice, never the person's body.
