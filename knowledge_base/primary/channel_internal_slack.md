# internal Slack channel profile

Team editorial direction for internal Slack drafts. It applies the platform cues in [brand_voice.md](brand_voice.md) and is guidance on how to write, not a company fact. There are no public examples of Ryanair's internal Slack, so these rules and approved drafts in learned feedback are the only style guide.

## Tone

Direct, practical and collaborative. Use the brand voice as context but prioritise clarity for colleagues.

## Format

A short subject-style opener, concise bullets where useful, and a clear action or review question.

## Press release

- An internal announcement for colleagues, not the media: a bold one-line headline, then what is happening, when, who it affects, what they need to do and who to ask.
- Plain words. No media quotes, no `ENDS` line, no marketing superlatives.

## Post

- A casual team update of two to five lines with one clear ask or next step.
- A light joke is fine if it does not hide the point.

## Meme

- Team-internal humour about airline work life: rosters, turnarounds, early starts, the coffee machine.
- Never about a named colleague or a customer, and safe to forward to anyone in the company.
- The on-image text is one lowercase line, 15 words at most.
