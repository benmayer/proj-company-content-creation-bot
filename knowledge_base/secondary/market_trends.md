# About this document

Industry context only. It contains no Ryanair company facts; those live in `knowledge_base/primary/`. Figures come from trade press and consumer bodies, listed in [sources.md](sources.md). Treat them as context for framing a post, not as numbers to quote as Ryanair's own.

# Capacity growth is led by low-cost carriers

- Trade coverage (Travel Extra, 2025) reports low-cost carriers growing capacity by about 8.7% in recent monthly comparisons, against 1.8% for mainline carriers.
- The same coverage reports IAG and Lufthansa trimming planned late-2025 capacity while Ryanair and Wizz Air kept expanding intra-European networks.
- Mainline carriers still hold roughly 60% of European seats, but their growth lags.
- Cirium data for 2024, as relayed by trade press, put Ryanair at about 13% of all European seats, and Wizz Air had moved from tenth to fourth largest in five years.

# Demand is healthy but past the post-pandemic surge

- Europe-linked air travel demand rose 5.6% year on year in Q3 2025: solid, but slower than the rebound years.
- Fares firmed up during 2025/26 after a weak prior year, according to results coverage (Travel Extra, 18 May 2026); check individual carriers' releases for numbers.

# Cost and supply pressures

- **Aircraft and engines:** Delivery delays at manufacturers and Pratt & Whitney engine problems constrain growth for several carriers. Wizz Air reported 30 aircraft grounded at 31 March 2026 (down from 42 a year earlier) and expects all to return by end-2027.
- **Fuel:** Mid-2026 coverage describes jet fuel spot prices spiking above $150 a barrel after Middle East conflict. Carriers with fuel hedges are insulated; unhedged carriers are not.
- **Air traffic control:** European ATC strikes and capacity limits are a recurring cause of delays and cancellations.
- **Taxes:** Airlines are pointing to rising EU environmental taxes as a cost headwind.

# Regulation: hand luggage and fee transparency

- In May 2025, 15 consumer organisations coordinated by BEUC filed a complaint against seven low-cost carriers (easyJet, Norwegian, Ryanair, Transavia, Volotea, Vueling, Wizz Air) over cabin-bag fees. They cite a 2014 EU Court of Justice ruling that hand luggage meeting reasonable weight and size limits cannot be surcharged.
- In November 2024, Spain's consumer ministry fined five budget airlines a combined €179 million over luggage charges. The European Commission has questioned whether Spain can do this nationally.
- The European Parliament endorsed, in July 2025, a passenger-rights proposal guaranteeing a free 7 kg cabin bag plus a personal item. Negotiations with member states were due to start in autumn 2025. Current status not confirmed: check before writing about it.

# Social and content trends in airline marketing

- Short-form video (Instagram Reels, TikTok) is the strongest reach driver for low-cost airlines in a 2025 comparative thesis on Ryanair, Vueling and easyJet.
- Airlines split roughly into humour-led (self-deprecating, meme-aware) and promotional or destination-storytelling styles. See [competitor_analysis.md](competitor_analysis.md).
- Trade commentary notes a trade-off between chasing viral formats and keeping a consistent voice, and warns that tone can drift when one person runs the account.
