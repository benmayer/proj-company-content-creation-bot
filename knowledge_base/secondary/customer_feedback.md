# About this document

Third-party survey and advocacy data on low-cost airline passengers in general. It is not Ryanair's own customer data. Several figures come from advocacy groups with a position, so use them as indicators of mood, not neutral statistics. Sources in [sources.md](sources.md).

# Main pain point: fees and luggage

- Euroconsumers polled about 4,000 consumers in Belgium, Italy, Spain and Portugal in June 2025: 73% believe luggage fees are used to mislead passengers and raise profit; 92% want standard size and weight rules; 90% want hand luggage included in the ticket price.
- Consumentenbond (Dutch BEUC member) found 86% of consumers think a small personal item or trolley bag should be in the basic fare.
- A 2025 survey of travellers to Mallorca found 57% felt misled by low-cost airlines over hidden fees, with 36% naming hand luggage fees as their top irritation. (Reported by Majorca Daily Bulletin; methodology not verified.)
- Which? found the cheapest advertised cabin-bag fee appeared on fewer than 1% of ~1,500 fares sampled.

# What this means for content

- The audience already expects budget airlines to be blunt about fees. Honesty about what a fare includes is a theme the audience cares about.
- Avoid claiming that fees are "transparent" or "included" unless the primary knowledge base confirms it.
- A human reviewer should check any post that touches luggage, refunds or delays, because these are active regulatory topics.
